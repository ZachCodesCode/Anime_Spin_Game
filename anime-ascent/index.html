Name the deep cut

Every day brings five anime questions, like “Name any Devil Fruit user”, “Name any Hashira” or “Name any Quirk”. You get 30 seconds to name one answer for each.

Obvious answers barely leave the ground. Deep cuts climb all the way up the mountain. Everyone gets the same seven questions, and you get one ascent per day.

FAQ

Is Anime Ascent like Krillion?

Yes. If you enjoy Krillion’s daily hunt for obscure answers, Anime Ascent is the same idea made for anime fans: name the rarest valid answer, with every question drawn from anime like One Piece, Naruto and Jujutsu Kaisen. It’s an independent fan project and isn’t affiliated with Krillion.

How is it scored?

Each answer lands in a tier, from Fodder (10 points) to Limit Break (100). Rarity is our editorial estimate of how obscure an answer is, not how strong the character is.

When do new questions come out?

A new set of five unlocks every day at midnight Eastern time.

Is it free?

Yes. It’s free to play in your browser, with no account or download.

What else is on Shonen Spin?

The main game: spin for factions, draft eight anime fighters into roles and see your chance against the Villain Gauntlet of Sukuna, Madara, Kaido and more. Play Shonen Spin.
