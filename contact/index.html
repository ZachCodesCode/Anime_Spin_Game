What to write to us about

  • Missing or wrong answers. Tell us the question (or the date of the ascent) and the answer you think should count.
  • Question ideas. We’re always looking for good “name any…” prompts that work across lots of anime.
  • Bugs. Let us know your device and browser, and what happened.
  • Privacy requests. See our privacy policy for what we store and how to ask us to delete it.
  • Rights holders and business enquiries. If you own content referenced on the site and have a concern, email us and we’ll respond promptly.
