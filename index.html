📖 HOW TO PLAY
  1. Spin. The reel lands on one of 30 crews/teams across 7 anime.
  2. Draft one fighter from that crew into an open role — you can only place a fighter in a role they're built for.
  3. Each pick auto-spins to the next crew. Use a Skip (3 total) to re-roll a crew you don't want.
  4. ELITE crews (legends) are rare; WEAK crews are common — so a dream team is hard to build.
  5. Fill all 8 roles, then CHALLENGE THE GAUNTLET for your % chance to beat Sukuna, Madara, Kaido & co.
  6. Hard mode: every fighter costs points by power. Stay under the cap — legends eat your whole budget.
