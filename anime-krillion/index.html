What is Anime Krillion?

Anime Krillion is a free daily anime quiz in the style of Krillion. Every day brings seven anime questions that work across all kinds of anime, like “Name any character with red hair”, “Name any Studio Ghibli film” or “Name any starter Pokémon”. You get 30 seconds to name one answer for each.

Obvious answers barely leave the ground. Deep cuts climb all the way up the mountain. Everyone gets the same seven questions, and you get one run per day. (The climbing mode is called Anime Ascent.)

How to play Anime Krillion

  1. 1 · Name one answerType any valid answer before the clock runs out. Wrong guesses are free while time is left.
  2. 2 · Rarer climbs higherEach answer’s rarity sets how far you climb. A perfect run, with the rarest answer every time, reaches the 5,000 m summit.
  3. 3 · Share your climbPost your altitude and tier squares, with no spoilers, and dare your friends to beat it.

Watch out for Too Clever: the “obscure” pick everyone reaches for scores less than an honest side character.

Reverse Anime Krillion

Prefer to read the crowd? Anime Descent is reverse Anime Krillion: seven different questions a day, and this time the most common answer wins. Say what everyone else would say and descend to the valley.

Anime Krillion FAQ

Is Anime Krillion like Krillion?

Yes. Anime Krillion takes Krillion’s daily hunt for obscure answers and makes it about anime, from One Piece, Naruto and Jujutsu Kaisen to deep cuts most fans have never heard of. It’s an independent fan project by Shonen Spin and isn’t affiliated with Krillion.

How is Anime Krillion scored?

Each answer lands in a tier, from Fodder (10 points) to Limit Break (100). Rarity is our editorial estimate of how obscure an answer is, not how strong the character is.

When do new questions come out?

A new set of seven unlocks every day at midnight Eastern time.

Is Anime Krillion free?

Yes. It’s free to play in your browser at shonenspin.com, with no account or download.

What else is on Shonen Spin?

The main game: spin for factions, draft eight anime fighters into roles and see your chance against the Villain Gauntlet of Sukuna, Madara, Kaido and more. Play Shonen Spin.
